# MiniCalendar interface exploration

Mode: Operate. Local review prototype; user delegated the first visual pass.
Three structural variants use the same sample events and sharing semantics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make private time and shared plans legible together. A calendar is the
primary working surface, with response actions beside the dates they affect.

OWN-WORLD: City cultural listings supply crisp sans typography, blue navigation,
white working areas, lilac group events and ochre friend events. Thin rules and
date anchors carry density. Colour identifies context; text identifies state.

STORY: See the week, find a time with people, confirm a plan, check who can see it.
Explicit available/busy/unknown cells are the signature interaction.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Week has a 208px navigation rail, a seven-day time grid and a
compact upcoming panel. Agenda moves navigation to the top and leads with date
groups. Planner leads with shared-time comparisons and a short decision queue.
New event and Find a time remain clear, familiar actions across variants.

FORM: Grounded direction 7, city cultural listings. seed:e450487bc1219eec.
The first pass is delegated, not a settled brand. Botanical-stage challenger
declined: retain consistency across repeated states. Streaming-wall challenger
declined: retain clear focus without concealing scheduling information. No
photographs or generated illustrations are required for this working app.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Review question

Which starting layout makes personal and shared planning clearest? The interactive
variants are the review artefacts; no final design selection has been made.
